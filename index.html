Questions, answered

How long does delivery take?

Most orders reach you in 3 to 7 working days after dispatch. We confirm your order and share tracking on WhatsApp.

Can I pay on delivery?

Yes. Cash on delivery is available on every order. You can also pay by UPI once we confirm your order.

What if the size doesn't fit?

Message us within 7 days of delivery and we'll arrange an exchange. Items must be unworn with tags attached.

How do I wash my clothes?

Machine wash clothes cold with similar colours and dry in shade. Wipe shoes and bags with a soft damp cloth and keep them out of direct sun.

Do you ship across India?

Yes, we deliver to most pin codes in India. Enter your pin code at checkout and we'll confirm.
